# Purpose

The National Incident Management System guides all levels of government,
nongovernmental organizations and the private sector to work together to
prevent, protect against, mitigate, respond to and recover from incidents.
It is applicable across every disaster type this platform models — it is not
specific to any one hazard.

# Shared Vocabulary and Structure

NIMS provides stakeholders across the whole community with shared
vocabulary, systems and processes so that agencies that do not normally work
together can integrate quickly during an incident. It defines the
operational systems that guide how personnel from different organizations
work together, rather than leaving coordination to be improvised on scene.

# Incident Command System

The Incident Command System (ICS) is the NIMS model for on-scene incident
management. It is a standardized, on-scene emergency management organization
used by public and private jurisdictions and agencies to organize
field-level incident-management operations, combining facilities, equipment,
personnel, procedures and communications within one common organizational
structure rather than parallel, agency-specific command chains.
